## Regressions

**R1H-1 · Severity 2 · Tries-used line clips the weekday: «el mar», «dt.»**
- Where: `src/components/VoiceDemo.tsx:22` (`weekday: "short"`) and `:424-427`; `es.ts:188-189`, `ca.ts:196-197`. Render `voice-tries-used.en.mobile.light.view.png` ("You can try again on Tue at 12:53 AM."). Tester's /es/ proof: ‘Podrás volver a probar el vie a las 17:00.’
- The moment: a Spanish visitor whose tries come back on a Tuesday reads «Podrás volver a probar el mar a las 17:00.» and wonders "¿el mar?". In Catalan, «Hi podràs tornar a provar dt. a les 17:00.»
- What happens: the sentence reuses the task cards' short weekday. In Spanish "mar" (martes) reads as "the sea", and Catalan gets "dl./dt./dc." with a full stop in the middle of a sentence. When the reset is later the same day, "on Mon" read on a Monday sounds like next week.
- Why it fails: GUIDELINES lens G (plain, natural copy in each language; never a literal or clipped form); HE-2 (match the real world).
- Fix: give the sentence its own long weekday, `new Intl.DateTimeFormat(language, { weekday: "long" })`, which gives "on Tuesday at 5:00 PM", «el martes a las 17:00» and «dimarts a les 17:00». Keep the short form on the cards. Optional, if the reset falls on today's date: EN `You’ve used this demo’s 3 tries. You can try again today at {time}.` / ES `Ya has usado los 3 intentos de esta demo. Podrás volver a probar hoy a las {time}.` / CA `Ja has fet servir els 3 intents d’aquesta demo. Hi podràs tornar a provar avui a les {time}.` (needs a native Catalan read). Spanish and Catalan also need «a la 1:00», not «a las/les 1:00», for one o'clock.
- Verify: on /es/ with `resetAt` on a Tuesday, the line contains "el martes"; on /ca/, "dimarts" and no "dt."; in English, "on Tuesday".
- Evidence: seen in the current render (English), and in the tester's /es/ string; the Spanish "mar" and Catalan "dt." are inferred from ICU's short weekdays.

**R1H-2 · Severity 2 · After a failed voice send, “Done” sits beside “Tap to talk”**
- Where: `src/components/VoiceDemo.tsx:619-633` (the `voice-submit` Done shows whenever `audio && !listening`); `audio` is never cleared on a failed send (`:368-381`). Render `voice-error-audio-length.en.mobile.light.panel.png`; the same layout appears in every `voice-error-invalid-audio-*` and `voice-error-too-large-*` render.
- The moment: "I haven't said anything yet. Done with what? Do I tap the green circle or the green button?"
- What happens: after a recording is refused, the panel shows the filled "Tap to talk" circle, its hint ("Your browser will ask to use the microphone"), and below them a filled "Done" that re-sends the same rejected recording. For audio-length and invalid-audio, that resend will fail again. This is the old ‘Create drafts’ resend, renamed to Done by WP27. Done now means "stop and send", which makes no sense when nothing is recording.
- Why it fails: lens C (one primary action per view, labelled with its outcome); HE-4 (one word, one meaning: Done is the stop-and-send button).
- Fix: remove the separate voice-mode Done. On a failed voice send, clear `audio` unless the request can be retrieved (`retryPending`), so only "Tap to talk" remains. When `retryPending` is set, WP29's filled `Try again` / `Reintentar` / `Torna-ho a provar` resends without using a try. Keep the typed-mode Done as it is. This fits inside WP29 and can merge with it.
- Verify: the `voice-error-audio-length` and `voice-error-invalid-audio-*` panels show one filled control ("Tap to talk"), and no button named Done unless listening; the `voice-offline`/`voice-timeout` voice variants show "Try again".
- Evidence: verified in code; seen in the current render.
