### Findings
**UX-2713 · partly fixed**
- Done: the failure has its own string, `meetingStopFailed`, in all three ARBs (ES «No se pudo parar. Sigue grabando: vuelve a pulsar Parar.»; EN and CA as the brief gives them), at `App/lib/screens/meeting_screen.dart:258-263`. `_stopFailed` keeps `_follow` on the recording screen (`:358-359`). The recorder keeps the session as `_unstopped`, so a second Stop finishes it (`App/lib/services/meeting_recorder.dart`, `stop()` and `_takeUnstopped`). The tester's widget test passes: own string, screen stays, Parar enabled, second press goes to «Preparando tus notas…».
- Not done: in the real recorder, «Sigue grabando» is false. `stop()` reaches its store work (`_closePiece`, `store.finish`, `stopQuiet`) only after `_close()` → `_shut()`, which has already stopped the microphone (`meeting_recorder.dart:470-486`: `engine.stop()`). `MeetingAudio.stop()` also clears the recording notification (`App/lib/services/meeting_audio.dart:192-212`). The catch then shows the meeting as recording again with a fixed `elapsed` and `level: 0`. So after a real failure, the screen shows «Grabando», «Puedes bloquear el teléfono: sigue grabando.», a frozen clock and a still ring, the snackbar says it's still recording, and the notification has gone. Nothing is being recorded. Someone who believes it can leave the phone recording the rest of the meeting, and lose that part. The tester's `_FailingStop` is scripted and stops nothing, so the test can't show this. No test drives a store failure inside the real recorder's `stop()`.
- Fix: say what's true, and leave «Parar» as the next step.
  - Snackbar `meetingStopFailed`: ES «No se pudo terminar de parar. Lo grabado está a salvo: vuelve a pulsar Parar.» / EN “Couldn’t finish stopping. What was recorded is safe: press Stop again.” / CA «No s’ha pogut acabar d’aturar. El que s’ha enregistrat és segur: torna a prémer Atura.»
  - While `_stopFailed` holds, the recording screen hides «Grabando» and «Puedes bloquear el teléfono: sigue grabando.».
  - Add a recorder test that makes `store.closePiece` or `finish` throw once: the second Stop then finishes the meeting, and nothing claims it's recording.
- Evidence: verified in code. The tester's widget test proves the string and the retry only over a scripted core.

**UX-2717 · fixed**
- Silent pieces are held (state `held`, numbered below 0) while no piece with sound has been kept (`meeting_recorder.dart` `_holds`; `meeting_store.dart` `closePiece(hold:)`). Once a piece with sound is kept, the held ones are left out and their files deleted (`_dropHeld`, MN-4).
- At Stop, a recording of `askWhenSilent` (60 s) or more that's empty and has held pieces becomes a stopped meeting (`stopQuiet` → `interrupted`). A shorter one is still discarded (`finish(keepHeld:false)`), with «No se oyó nada…» (`meeting_screen.dart:269-273`). The way a recording that ended by itself is handled also uses `keepHeld:false`, so it's unchanged.
- The dialog (`meeting_screen.dart` `_askQuiet`): «Casi no se oyó nada. ¿Hacer notas igualmente?», a filled «Hacer las notas» (`meetingMakeNotes`, reused from P322) and «Descartar» in `colorScheme.error`. «Hacer las notas» → `finishMeeting`, which renumbers the held pieces 0..n-1 as pending, and the screen follows on to «Preparando tus notas…». «Descartar» → `discardMeeting`, then `_leave()`.
- Render `after-stop.quiet-question.es.light.1x`: the question in two lines; «Descartar» (red) stacked above the filled «Hacer las notas», about 32 dp apart. AUDIT measured no problems; TalkBack reads «Alerta», the question, «Descartar», «Hacer las notas».
- Tests (real controller, recorder, store and uploader over fakes): 10 quiet minutes are kept with every file on disk and nothing sent; Hacer las notas sends pieces 0..n-1 and finish lastIndex n-1; Descartar removes rows and files; 30 s is still dropped; sound in the third piece keeps pieces 0 and 1 from that point, and the silent files are deleted.
- Owed: the phone check from the plan (a quiet real room at 2–3 m).

### Regressions
**R1-P324-1 · Severity 2 · Quiet meeting's card says it cut out before Parar**
- Where: `App/lib/widgets/meeting_banners.dart:335-350` (`MeetingStoppedCard`, `meetingStoppedCut`), shown for the quiet meeting by `_After` (`App/lib/screens/meeting_screen.dart:638-653`) and by the Notes tab's stopped line. It's visible behind the dialog in the render `after-stop.quiet-question.es.light.1x`.
- The moment: they press «Parar». They close the dialog by mistake (tapping outside, or Back; `showDialog` defaults to `barrierDismissible: true`), or leave and come back later. They read «Grabación de hoy, 10:15 · 10 min. Se cortó antes de que pulsaras Parar.» "But I did press Parar. Did it break? Was something lost?"
- Why it fails: UN-7, errors and states say what happened; recurring problem 9, copy borrowed from another context. The package now uses the `interrupted` phase, and its card, for a meeting the person stopped themselves.
- Fix: when a stopped meeting kept no piece with sound but holds pieces (the quiet stop), its line says what happened: ES «{recording} · {length}. Casi no se oyó nada.» / EN “{recording} · {length}. Almost nothing was heard.” / CA «{recording} · {length}. Gairebé no s’ha sentit res.» The same choices follow. To tell it apart, either the model exposes `empty` plus held pieces, or `stopQuiet` records it.
- Verify: re-render `after-stop.quiet-question` with the dialog dismissed, and render `notes-lines` with a quiet stopped meeting. Neither may show «Se cortó antes de que pulsaras Parar».
- Evidence: verified in code; seen in the current render (behind the barrier).

### Memory notes
- Recurring type, **a scripted fake hides a false state claim**: ScriptedMeetings' throwing `stop()` stops nothing, while the real recorder's `stop()` stops the mic before it can fail. Before trusting a scripted UI test for any "still X" copy, check the real service's order of side effects.
- Recurring type 9 again: reusing the `interrupted` phase for a user-initiated quiet stop brought along the MN-12 card copy «Se cortó antes de que pulsaras Parar».
- Meeting pieces: there's now a `held` state. Silent pieces are kept, numbered below 0, while no piece has sound. They're renumbered 0..n-1 on «Hacer las notas» and dropped once a piece with sound is kept. `askWhenSilent` = 60 s.
