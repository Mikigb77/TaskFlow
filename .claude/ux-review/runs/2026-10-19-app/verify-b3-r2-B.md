**R2B-1 · Severity 1 · At 2×, the one «Sin hora» row is cut**
- Where: `App/lib/screens/day_timeline_screen.dart:~270` (pinned area, `maxHeight: space.maxHeight / 3`), Día view, 2× text, one date-only task. Render `calendar/timeline.untimed.es.light.2x.png`.
- The moment: «¿Se ha roto? La tarjeta de la factura se corta por la mitad.»
- What happens: at 2× on 360×800 the 3-line date header leaves ~575 dp; a third is ~192 dp, so the single row is cut through its «Hoy · Prioridad Media» line, and the hours begin right under the cut with no edge between them. The brief meant the *next* row to show cut; here the only row is, and its «Hoy» repeats the date in the header anyway.
- Why it fails: Gestalt common region (Palmer 1992): the cut card bleeds into the timeline with no boundary; minimalist design (Nielsen 1994): «Hoy» on a row under a header that names the day.
- Fix (subtract first): pass `showDate: false` to the untimed `TaskTile`/`LandedTaskTile` on the Día view, so a date-only row shows no date line (TaskTile already does this for a dated row without time) and fits in the third at 2×; add a 1 dp `outlineVariant` divider under the pinned area so a cut reads as an edge. No copy.
- Verify: `calendar/timeline.untimed.es.light.2x` shows the whole «Pagar la factura de la luz» row above a line, then 08:00.
- Evidence: seen in a current render; Android 14's non-linear scaling shrinks the header on a phone, so check on the emulator lane at 2.0.
