# What the first device captures showed (2026-10-19)

The agent that built the lane ran `capture.py` on `TaskFlow_Review` from `dev` at 6ed07ef, which is before batches b4 and b5 of run 2026-10-18-commands. It reported these problems and did not fix them.

All of this is **emulator evidence**: no physical phone and no real voice. The captures are in `App/build/ux/device/`, listed in its `INDEX.md`. The next review should confirm each item there before filing it.

1. **Fixed on 2026-10-19 by the main session.** Both launcher shortcuts did nothing.
   - They're now published from code (`CaptureShortcuts.kt`), and the static XML is gone.
   - On the emulator, «Dictar una tarea» opens the sheet listening and «Escribir una tarea» opens the form (captures `launcher.shortcuts.*`, `shortcut-*.after-tap.*`).
   - What it was:
   - The launcher logs "Shortcut could not be started", and Android registered the target as `cmp=@2131689645/…`.
   - Cause: `android:targetPackage="@string/taskflow_package"` in `App/android/app/src/main/res/xml/capture_shortcuts.xml`. Static shortcuts need the literal package name.
   - The staging build has its own package (`.staging`), so a fix must give each build its own value.
2. **A task added on the server can take minutes to get its reminder.** It happened twice in the full run, even after reopening and restarting the app. It wasn't clear whether the app or emulator load caused it; phone check.
3. **A second microphone denial isn't detected** when the app records audio instead of using on-device recognition (as for Catalan here).
   - The sheet keeps the first banner and offers no way to Settings.
   - In `voice_task_screen.dart`, `_startRecording` maps the recorder's check only to granted or denied, never to blocked.
4. **The countdown doesn't count.** At 00:57 it still says «Quedan 10 segundos».
5. **When the 60-second limit passes with nothing heard,** the sheet silently resets to «Empezar a hablar».
6. **The microphone permission flow:**
   - nothing explains why before Android's dialog;
   - the denied banner says «escribe abajo», but there's no field below, only the «Escribir» button;
   - «Abrir Ajustes» lands on App info, not on the microphone switch.
7. **Back from a shared task's form saves it as an undated task, with no message.** It was confirmed on the server. «el viernes» also stays in the title, which may be by design on the free tier.
8. **The too-long share message is cut off:** «Comparte un texto más co…».
9. **The calendar permission:**
   - nothing happens after the first denial;
   - at 2× text the connect button is below the fold;
   - after allowing, the first option offered is «Ocultar eventos del calendario».
10. **Tapping a reminder opens the calendar's day, not the task.** That one time the calendar also showed «Sin conexión» while the backend was up.
11. **Right after alarms are allowed,** Recordatorios briefly shows «Permiso del sistema desactivado» with the switch off.
12. **At 2× text and 360 dp:**
    - the shortcut labels are cut: «Dicta un…», «Escriu u…»;
    - the Catalan widget label loses «tasca»;
    - the week strip's «dom» becomes «do».
13. **The launcher icon** isn't adaptive. It shows small in a white circle and doesn't match the in-app logo.
14. **The text shortcut, started with the launcher's flags while the app is open,** lands on the calendar instead of the form, with «Sin guardar · 4». Re-checked after the fix of 1.
    - Real taps from the launcher land right: the launcher, not the app, is in front then.
    - The four unsaved captures are probably the test's own shortcut starts, one after another.
    - Check on a phone.
